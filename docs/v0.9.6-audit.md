## 5. Audit gates (unchanged, restated)

From `feedback_wiki_scraping_pattern.md`:

1. **Spike threshold: ≥90% coverage on the 15-item spike** before launching the full scrape. If under 90%, fix the resolver or seed OVERRIDES first.
2. **5%+ random-sample manual audit of `c:search` and `d:html` hits.** These are the high-risk paths — every page they touch must be eyeballed against the real wiki article to catch silent mis-resolutions (e.g. "Great white shark" landing on "Great white shark model" furniture page).
3. **`missing-acnh.txt` iteration loop until honest.** Items that genuinely have no wiki page get logged with a one-line reason; items that the chain can't auto-resolve get promoted to OVERRIDES. No item silently disappears.
4. **Coverage target**: an honest 100% means "every item either has a real icon OR appears in `missing-acnh.txt` with a documented reason." Cross-game routing uplift to other games is expected as a side effect (same as v0.9.5).

## 7. Risk register

What's different about ACNH vs the prior two scrapes:

- **Biggest catalog (330 items)** — even at 600ms `DELAY_MS`, a full scrape of 103 items is ~10 minutes minimum. Not a problem, just plan for it.
- **Variation suffixes** — ACNH bugs/fish wiki articles often list "Variation 1/2/3" image options. Current resolver pulls `pageimages.original` which is the canonical infobox image, so this *shouldn't* matter, but the c:search/d:html paths could surface variant pages. Worth eyeballing during the 5% audit.
- **Fossil multi-part density** — 56 fossil pieces is the largest single-category gap we've scraped. Most should collapse to ~17 parent species via OVERRIDES, but expect this PR's OVERRIDES block to be 2-3× longer than v0.9.5's.
- **Sea-creature coverage from scratch** — ACNL sea-creatures resolved through `(deep-sea creature)` disambig in v0.9.5. Same pattern applies to ACNH's 10 sea-creature gaps, but the catalog is different (gigas-giant-clam, slate-pencil-urchin, sweet-shrimp) and may carry novel name drift. No new resolver work expected, but allocate spike slots.
- **Art real-vs-fake split is NOT a v0.9.6 concern** — that's a feature gate for v1.0. Icons are one-per-piece either way; the 9 art gaps are paintings/statues that simply have no icon mapped yet.
